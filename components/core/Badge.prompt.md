Compact uppercase label for service lines, statuses, and content categories.

```jsx
<Badge variant="primary">Tax</Badge>
<Badge variant="accent">Advisory</Badge>
<Badge variant="sky">Audit</Badge>
<Badge variant="dark">15 Anos</Badge>
<Badge variant="subtle-green" size="sm">Consultoria</Badge>
<Badge variant="white">Premium</Badge>
```

**Variants:**
- `primary` — green. For Tax service labels and primary status.
- `accent` — gold. For Advisory labels.
- `sky` — blue mist. For Audit labels.
- `dark` — black pill. High contrast on light backgrounds.
- `subtle-green/gold/blue` — tinted, for use in cards or tables.
- `white` — semi-transparent white. For badges on dark/green surfaces.

**Sizes:** `sm`, `md` (default), `lg`
